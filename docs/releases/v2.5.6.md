# diskOS Disco! 2.5.6

Pick several songs at once, plus fixes for the queue and for Bluetooth.

> **Testing:** the queue fixes were tried on a Disc. Picking several items was checked on a computer only, and
> the Bluetooth changes were not fully tested on a Disc (AAC/LDAC not at all, no such headphones at hand). If you use LDAC or AAC headphones, please
> [tell us how it goes](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/13).

## New: pick several items

**Swipe sideways across a song, album, artist or folder** to start picking. Tap more items to tick them (or swipe
across them too); the title shows how many are picked. At the bottom: **Play**, **Queue** and **More** (Shuffle,
Add to playlist, Add to Favourites, Select all). Holding an item while picking opens More. Back, or unticking
everything, ends picking. Tapping and holding work as before when you're not picking. Play doesn't touch your queue.

A folder counts as every song inside it. Start the swipe a little in from the screen's edge: the edges stay reserved
for the back swipe.

## Queue

- **Queued songs play again when it's their turn.** Since 2.5.1 the queue could skip its turn at a song change,
  or show "Couldn't start queued song". Fixed.
- **Start straight from the queue.** With nothing playing, add songs or a folder to the queue and press Play (on the
  Queue screen or Music) or Next: the queue plays from its first song, in order. In the Shuffle play mode it plays in
  a random order. When the queue is done, playback stops.

## Bluetooth

- **Songs no longer stop a few seconds after you pick a playlist** ([#13](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/13)).
  When Disco moves the sound back to your headphones and the song stops by itself, it presses play once. If music
  still doesn't move, or after an AAC/LDAC switch, it shows "Playback paused - press Play to resume" instead of
  acting again. It only watches for a few seconds, for the same headphones and song, and never after you press
  Play/Pause yourself.
- **Connecting is more reliable:** the search for devices stops before connecting, and headphones that are already
  paired are no longer paired again.
- **Your headphones reconnect by themselves.** When Bluetooth comes on, at start-up or with the switch, the Disc
  calls the headphones it last played to, a few times.
- No more stutter while a connection is being set up.

## Also included

- **Fixes from a code review:** picking several items now uses the exact songs everywhere: one CUE/ISO track stays
  one track in the queue, an album picked under one artist no longer pulls in another artist's album with the same
  name, folders with CUE/ISO files keep every track, and very large albums are no longer cut off. Add to Favourites
  only reports what was really saved and keeps the selection when something fails. A queue start that fails no
  longer loses queued songs. Select all in Files only ticks folders and songs.
- **Squash lists:** rows near the top and bottom edge no longer light up orange while you scroll.
- Docs and website: notes about switching between diskOS builds, starting from stock V2.57, and the Windows app.
- **Windows app:** the install now downloads the latest release each time (checked against its checksums), unless
  you pick an `mq_ui` file of your own; offline, it offers the version built in. Its start screen says Windows is
  unsupported but working as intended (confirmed by users), to start from stock V2.57 and what to do after
  switching from another diskOS build.

## Installing and updating

- **Already on diskOS Disco! 1.4.1 or newer?** Update from the SD card: download `mq_ui`, sign it with your own
  keys and copy the `diskos-update` folder to the card ([how](../UPDATING.md)). Your settings, music and stats stay.
- **New install?** Start from **FiiO's stock V2.57 firmware** (recommended). Installing over upstream diskOS or
  another diskOS build is not supported and can be buggy; restore stock first. See [Install](../../README.md#install).
- **Windows:** Unsupported, but working as intended: confirmed several times by users, installs and SD updates included. Start from stock V2.57 there too.

## After updating

- **Settings > System > About** shows **2.5.6**.
- The new version starts as a **trial**: play some music or tap **Keep** to keep it. If it doesn't start properly,
  the Disc goes back to the previous version by itself, or choose **Go back**.
- Something odd? Hold **Volume Up** from power-on to start FiiO's own interface once, and restart to come back.

<summary>Technical notes</summary>

Multi-select: `multipick.c`, shared by the Library and the folder browser (rows marked per item index,
survives row recycling; swipe needs >= 36 px sideways, < 22 px vertical, starting >= 72 px from the edges).
Queue: queue lookups wait up to 1.5 s for the player's database write (2.5.1 made them give up at once); a fresh
start path when no current song can be identified. Bluetooth: a 10 s resume guard bound to route MAC, player generation and track identity, cancelled at EOF and by
on-screen transport; after `ui_route_bt` one play (only after a successful send), else and after the 06b3 codec
upgrade a hint only; no per-device codec downgrade is stored. `scan off` before pair/connect, pair only if unpaired;
`bt_last` reconnect (3 tries, 8 s cap each); connection check on a worker thread. Multi-select actions share one
exact-ID list (`queue_add_ids`, `mdb_folder_ids`, artist-scoped album collection without a cap, one cleanup path);
a fresh queue start commits its new order only after playback starts. Queue tests extended (busy database, nothing playing). Built with
the GCC 11.2 MIPS toolchain; build record in `payload/mq_ui.build.json`.

</details>
