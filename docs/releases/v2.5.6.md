# diskOS Disco! 2.5.6

Fixes for the queue and for Bluetooth.

> **Testing:** the queue fixes were tried on a Disc. The Bluetooth changes were not fully tested on a Disc,
> and AAC/LDAC not at all (no such headphones at hand). If you use LDAC or AAC headphones, please
> [tell us how it goes](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/13).

## Queue

- **Queued songs play again when it's their turn.** Since 2.5.1 the queue could skip its turn at a song change,
  or show "Couldn't start queued song". Fixed.
- **Start straight from the queue.** With nothing playing, add songs or a folder to the queue and press Play (on the
  Queue screen or Music) or Next: the queue plays from its first song, in order. In the Shuffle play mode it plays in
  a random order. When the queue is done, playback stops.

## Bluetooth

- **Songs no longer stop a few seconds after you pick a playlist** ([#13](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/13)).
  After Disco moves the sound to your headphones or switches codec, it checks that music really carries on, and
  presses play once if it stopped by itself.
- **LDAC and AAC that don't hold.** If music won't continue after switching to LDAC, those headphones go back to SBC
  ("Bluetooth codec unstable - using SBC") and Disco remembers it: next time it tries AAC, then SBC. Other
  headphones are not affected.
- **Connecting is more reliable:** the search for devices stops before connecting, and headphones that are already
  paired are no longer paired again.
- **Your headphones reconnect by themselves.** When Bluetooth comes on, at start-up or with the switch, the Disc
  calls the headphones it last played to, a few times.
- No more stutter while a connection is being set up.

## Also included

- **Squash lists:** rows near the top and bottom edge no longer light up orange while you scroll.
- Docs and website: notes about switching between diskOS builds, starting from stock V2.57, and the Windows app.
- The Windows app's start screen now explains it is unsupported, to start from stock V2.57 and what to do after
  switching from another diskOS build.

## Installing and updating

- **Already on diskOS Disco! 1.4.1 or newer?** Update from the SD card: download `mq_ui`, sign it with your own
  keys and copy the `diskos-update` folder to the card ([how](../UPDATING.md)). Your settings, music and stats stay.
- **New install?** Start from **FiiO's stock V2.57 firmware** (recommended). Installing over upstream diskOS or
  another diskOS build is not supported and can be buggy; restore stock first. See [Install](../../README.md#install).
- **Windows:** the Windows app is shared as-is and unsupported. Start from stock V2.57 there too.

## After updating

- **Settings > System > About** shows **2.5.6**.
- The new version starts as a **trial**: play some music or tap **Keep** to keep it. If it doesn't start properly,
  the Disc goes back to the previous version by itself, or choose **Go back**.
- Something odd? Hold **Volume Up** from power-on to start FiiO's own interface once, and restart to come back.

<details>
<summary>Technical notes</summary>

Queue: queue lookups wait up to 1.5 s for the player's database write (2.5.1 made them give up at once); a fresh
start path when no current song can be identified. Bluetooth: a 10 s resume guard after `ui_route_bt` and after the
06b3 codec upgrade (play once after 3.5 s without progress; for a codec switch, then SBC and a per-device
`btcx_<mac>` downgrade); `scan off` before pair/connect, pair only if unpaired; `bt_last` reconnect (3 tries, 8 s
cap each); connection check on a worker thread. Queue tests extended (busy database, nothing playing). Built with
the GCC 11.2 MIPS toolchain; build record in `payload/mq_ui.build.json`.

</details>
