# diskOS Disco! 2.1.0

An SD update from 1.4.1 or later: no reflash. [Download](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/releases/latest) ·
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md)

## New

**Listening Stats.** Your music, in numbers: how long you listened this week, month or year and how that compares,
your top artists, albums and songs, the song on repeat, and a 24-hour listening clock round the rim. Open it from
All apps or Shortcuts, or add **Stats** to the Disco Menu. It counts from the day you install 2.1.0.
[More](../DISCO_TOUR.md#listening-stats)

**SongSphere** (separate app). Place your albums and artists on a mood sphere, from Calm to Energetic and from Dark
to Bright, then drag to a mood and play: up to 40 songs from near that spot, in Shuffle, kept as a playlist if you
like. [Install it](../SONGSPHERE_INSTALL.md)

**Immersive, your way, in every theme.** Display > Text & Layout > Immersive: Vinyl, CD, or None (the plain cover,
standing still). Immersive Lyrics turns the lyrics and their dark shade on or off.

## Fixed

- A touch on a dark screen no longer presses what's under it: it only wakes the screen. Lock the screen in a pocket
  with the power button (a one-time tip says so).
- By Artist plays exactly the songs the list shows, collaborations included, and Next stays with that artist.
- Album rows show the album artist (or Various Artists).
- Hints fit their text at every size; installed apps open their landing page from All apps and Shortcuts.
- Signing two updates within the same minute works.

## Install

Windows: the Windows app's **Update** page. Other systems: [Updating](../UPDATING.md).
