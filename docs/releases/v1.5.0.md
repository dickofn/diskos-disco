## Boomshine

A chain-reaction game for the round screen. Dots drift around; tap once to set off a blast, and every dot it
catches bursts too. Catch enough to clear the level, across twenty levels. Two looks: **NeoN** (bright rings on
black) and **Pop!** (soft pastels on deep blue).

- **Built in**, nothing to install. Add **Boomshine** in Disco Options > Disco Menu, or open it from All apps or a
  Shortcut.
- Your music keeps playing; the game is silent.
- Best score and level are saved. Scores from the earlier separate Boomshine app are carried over.
- Hold Volume Up for 3 seconds to close it if it ever stops responding.

[How to play](../DISCO_TOUR.md#boomshine)

## Apps in the navigation circle

- **Any installed app** (Disc Shelf, future apps) can now be added to the navigation circle in Disco Options >
  Disco Menu, next to the built-in sections. Entries keep their place as you install or remove other apps.
- **Landing pages.** Choosing Boomshine or an app in the circle shows its landing page first: a picture, a short
  description and one button. Nothing launches until you tap it, so browsing the circle is always safe.
- Apps without their own page get a tidy default ("Installed app" and **Launch**). App makers can set the line,
  description and button text in `app.conf` ([details](../DISCO_TOUR.md#apps-in-the-menu)).
- Folders without anything to run are no longer listed, and Album Roulette and Boomshine never show up twice.
