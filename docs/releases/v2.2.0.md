# diskOS Disco! 2.2.0

An SD update from 1.4.1 or later: no reflash. [Download](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/releases/latest) ·
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md)

## New

**SongSphere, built in.** Music by mood, now part of diskOS: hold an album or an artist in the Library and choose
**Mood**, or tap the mood pill in Music's track popup to place the song playing. Then open SongSphere, drag to a mood
and play. If you used the 2.1 add-on, your placements carry over. Turn it off in Settings > Playback > SongSphere.
[More](../DISCO_TOUR.md#songsphere)

**Apps in the circle.** A new Disco Menu section with up to five apps you choose, each with its icon.
[More](../DISCO_TOUR.md#apps)

**Listening Stats, refreshed.** Swipe through weeks, months and years; your top 5 artists, albums and songs, with a
tap for the top 15; and a Wrapped for each #1. [More](../DISCO_TOUR.md#listening-stats)

## Install

Windows: the Windows app's **Update** page. Other systems: [Updating](../UPDATING.md).
