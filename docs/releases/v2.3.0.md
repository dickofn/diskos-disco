# diskOS Disco! 2.3.0

An SD update from 1.4.1 or later: no reflash. [Download](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/releases/latest) ·
[changelog](../../CHANGELOG.md) · [2.2.2 release notes](v2.2.2.md)

- **Drop the needle:** hold the title and the cover drops onto the record in Immersive, spinning in; a tap lifts it off.
- **Controls, your way:** Disco Options > Music Screen > Controls: Buttons, Auto-hide, or Gestures (double tap to
  play/pause, swipe right for next, left for previous).
- **Hold to scrub:** hold the progress line or arc, and the time follows your finger, large.
- **Listening Stats, bolder:** a straight period picker with arrows, the time on one line, bigger text everywhere,
  and new Highlights: your longest streak, busiest day and average listening day.
- **Quick Settings round the edge:** seven big buttons on the screen's curve, Wi-Fi in the middle.
- **Battery:** when it will run out, or when it'll be full, on a cleaner ring.
- **Library:** more room for long names.
- **Search:** up to five artists, the biggest first.

Quick Settings now holds seven items. If you had eight, the last one other than Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Settings is left out.

## Install

Windows: the Windows app's **Update** page. Other systems: [Updating](../UPDATING.md).
