# diskOS Disco! 2.5.5

> **Not tested on a Disc yet.** This release was built and checked on a computer only. If anything misbehaves,
> the SD update rolls back by itself, or choose **Go back**; 2.5.4 stays available on the releases page.

## Experimental: Thai, Vietnamese and Korean

Song titles, artists, albums and lyrics in more languages, in every theme:

- **Thai** now shows instead of empty boxes. The Disc can't stack Thai marks the way phones do, so tone marks are
  placed above the vowels: always readable, sometimes a little higher than you're used to.
- **Vietnamese** letters with accents now show.
- **Korean**: the 2,350 everyday syllables, at the same smaller size as Chinese and Japanese.

Not yet: Hebrew and Arabic (right-to-left) and Indian scripts. Please
[tell us](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues) how your music looks, with a screenshot if you can.

## Fixes and small things

- **Covers on fuller cards.** Covers are cached until the SD card has less than 500 MB free (was 1 GB), so Cover
  Flow and lists show covers on nearly full cards.
- **Restart the battery totals.** On the Battery page, hold the "Since full charge" line: playing and screen-on time
  start again from now ("Since reset" until the next full charge).
- Docs: the how-to matches the 2.5.4 Display menu, a showcase video on the website, and clearer install advice.

## Installing and updating

- **Already on diskOS Disco! 1.4.1 or newer?** Update from the SD card: download `mq_ui`, sign it with your own
  keys and copy the `diskos-update` folder to the card ([how](../UPDATING.md)). Your settings, music and stats stay.
- **New install?** Start from **FiiO's stock V2.57 firmware** (recommended). Installing over upstream diskOS or
  another diskOS build is not supported and can be buggy; restore stock first. See [Install](../../README.md#install).
- **Windows:** the Windows app is shared as-is and unsupported. Start from stock V2.57 there too.

## After updating

- **Settings > System > About** shows **2.5.5**.
- The new version starts as a **trial**: play some music or tap **Keep** to keep it. If it doesn't start properly,
  the Disc goes back to the previous version by itself, or choose **Go back**.
- Something odd? Hold **Volume Up** from power-on to start FiiO's own interface once, and restart to come back.
