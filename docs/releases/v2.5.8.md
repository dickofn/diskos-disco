# diskOS Disco! 2.5.8

Bluetooth fixes for LDAC and AAC headphones ([#13](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/13)), and a less confusing back swipe.

> **Testing:** checked with automatic tests and on a computer. Not yet tried on a Disc with LDAC or AAC headphones
> (none at hand). Reports are welcome on [issue #13](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/13).

## Bluetooth

- **No more stop a few seconds into a song with LDAC/AAC.** Switching to LDAC or AAC renegotiates the Bluetooth link,
  and doing that while music played stopped the song after about 6 seconds. Now, when the sound moves to LDAC/AAC
  headphones, the music waits until the switch is done and then starts on the final codec. You may hear a second or
  two of silence at the start instead. If the switch doesn't happen within 8 seconds, the music plays on SBC. Pressing
  Play/Pause yourself ends the wait at once.
- **No more short dropouts from device searches.** Searching for devices shares the Disc's small radio with Bluetooth
  audio and caused split-second gaps during playback. While sound goes to your headphones, the Bluetooth page now lists
  your known devices without searching; you can still search when you want to.

## Navigation

- **No back arrow where Back goes nowhere.** On Home (the Music screen in Disco and Squash), a back swipe showed the
  back arrow although there was nothing to go back to. The arrow now only appears when the swipe will actually do
  something, in every theme.

## Installing and updating

- **Already on diskOS Disco! 1.4.1 or newer?** Update from the SD card: download `mq_ui`, sign it with your own
  keys and copy the `diskos-update` folder to the card ([how](../UPDATING.md)). Your settings, music and stats stay.
- **New install?** Start from **FiiO's stock V2.57 firmware** (recommended). Installing over upstream diskOS or
  another diskOS build is not supported and can be buggy; restore stock first. See [Install](../../README.md#install).
- **Windows:** Unsupported, but working as intended: confirmed several times by users, installs and SD updates included.

## After updating

- **Settings > System > About** shows **2.5.8**.
- The new version starts as a **trial**: play some music or tap **Keep** to keep it. If it doesn't start properly,
  the Disc goes back to the previous version by itself, or choose **Go back**.

<summary>Technical notes</summary>

`ui_route_bt` no longer resumes at once when an AAC/LDAC upgrade is armed: `route_hold_tick` plays after the 06b3
upgrade is sent plus 1.5 s settle, or after 8 s on SBC (`ui_bt_codec_upgrade_cancel`); on-screen transport and a route
change cancel the hold. The codec probe ticks every 0.7 s (was 2 s). The Bluetooth page uses `scan_kick` (enumerate
known devices, no inquiry) instead of discovery while `ui_route_mac()` is set. The back-swipe bubble is hidden when
`screen_back_leads_nowhere()` (Home, empty history, no launcher/overlay open). Test: bt_bulk_favourites_test.py
(route hold). Built with the GCC 11.2 MIPS toolchain; build record in `payload/mq_ui.build.json`.
