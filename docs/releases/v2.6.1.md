# diskOS Disco! 2.6.1

Fixes for Bluetooth headphones dropping during playback, and for the Disc powering off while in use.

## Bluetooth

- **Headphones no longer drop mid-song.** Every few seconds Disco checks the headphones' audio link. When the Disc
  was busy (a cover loading, a skip, or simply the screen being off), that check could answer late, and 2.6.0 took a
  late answer for "the link is gone". It then set the connection up again, which restarts the player's Bluetooth
  service: the headphones dropped, reconnected and switched codec again. Now a late answer changes nothing; only
  three checks in a row that really find no link (9 seconds) count as a disconnect.
- **Music starts on AAC/LDAC right away.** Just after headphones connect, Disco still has to switch them from SBC to
  AAC or LDAC, and switching stops a playing song. Now a song you start in that moment waits a second or two ("Getting
  Bluetooth ready...") and then plays on the right codec from the start, instead of starting and pausing a few
  seconds in. Pressing Play again starts it at once.
- **Skipping during the short codec-switch pause** (first or second song after connecting) no longer leaves the
  music paused on the new song: the pause follows you to the song you land on and plays it once the switch is done.

## Idle power-off

- **No more power-off while the Disc is in use without playing a song of its own**
  ([#17](https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco/issues/17)). Disco's idle power-off only looked at songs playing on the
  Disc, so it switched off in **Bluetooth DAC** mode and the other working modes (USB DAC, BT streaming, AirPlay, USB
  storage), and while **charging**. It now only counts as idle in Playback mode, with nothing playing, and the Disc
  neither plugged in nor charging: a full battery still on the charger keeps it on too.

## Never stopped on a guess

- Disco's idea of "playing" is a guess from the song's position moving, and it can miss music that is in fact
  playing. Before any action that would stop or interrupt music because it "isn't playing" - **idle power-off**,
  setting up the Bluetooth route, the play/pause around a codec switch - Disco now also asks the audio system whether
  sound is going out. If it is, the Disc stays on and the music isn't touched.

Everything else is as in [2.6.0](v2.6.0.md).

> **Testing:** the Bluetooth fix is covered by an automatic test that fails on 2.6.0 and passes on 2.6.1. Neither fix
> was tried on a Disc before release.

## Installing and updating

- **Already on diskOS Disco! 1.4.1 or newer?** Update from the SD card: download `mq_ui`, sign it with your own
  keys and copy the `diskos-update` folder to the card ([how](../UPDATING.md)). Your settings, music and stats stay.
- **New install?** Start from **FiiO's stock V2.57 firmware** (recommended). Installing over upstream diskOS or
  another diskOS build is not supported and can be buggy; restore stock first. See [Install](../../README.md#install).
- **Windows:** Unsupported, but working as intended: confirmed several times by users, installs and SD updates included.

## After updating

- **Settings > System > About** shows **2.6.1**.
- The new version starts as a **trial**: play some music or tap **Keep** to keep it. If it doesn't start properly,
  the Disc goes back to the previous version by itself, or choose **Go back**.

<summary>Technical notes</summary>

`bt_autoroute_poll_cb` now calls `run_cap_to` directly: a timed-out `bluealsa-cli list-pcms` probe (-1) returns
without touching any state; only three consecutive completed probes without an A2DP PCM clear the auto-route latch,
cancel a pending codec switch and call `ui_route_forget("link gone")`. In 2.6.0 two timed-out probes (read as empty by
`run_cap_bounded`) forgot the route, and the following re-route's 06c1 restarted bluetoothd/BlueALSA. Test:
`ui/tests/bt_autoroute_test.py`. A skip during the codec hold re-takes the song identity for 3 s
(`ui_route_hold_follow`) instead of cancelling the hold; `ui_audio_running()` (kernel `/proc/asound/card*/pcm*p/sub*/status`
"state: RUNNING", and BlueALSA's "Running:" when it reports one) gates idle power-off, the route's was-playing, the
codec gate, the hold end and play deferral; a list play or Play on a paused song while the codec
upgrade is pending waits for it (`play_defer_tick`: confirmed, 10 s, or the route gone; a second Play starts at once); the player-restart path and `ui_route_analog` now drop the
route's codec switch, hold and guard explicitly. Idle power-off: `power_tick` gets "busy" = playing, Music Assistant playing, a
working mode other than Playback (`ui_get_source_mode() != 0`), charging (`usage_charging()`, a positive charge
current) or plugged in (`usage_plugged_in()`: any non-battery entry in /sys/class/power_supply with online=1, read
at most every 5 s). Tests: `ui/tests/power_plugged_test.py`. Built with the GCC 11.2 MIPS toolchain; build record in `payload/mq_ui.build.json`.
