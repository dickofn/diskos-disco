Your Disc, your way.

A new interface for the Disc's round screen. The album you're playing fills it, and everything else is yours to arrange, recolour, and fill with apps: play by mood, see your year in music, or set off a chain reaction between songs.

Music

New in 2.2

The cover is the interface.

The album fills the screen, sharp. Over it: the time and the weather, the title and artist, a progress line you drag to seek, and play controls on an arc that follows the round edge.

  • Tap the title for Favourite, Album, Artist, Queue, Lyrics, Info, and the song's mood.
  • Hold the title for the full-screen cover: a spinning vinyl or CD, or the plain cover, with or without lyrics.
  • Line or arc: progress under the title, or all the way around the rim.
  • Readable on any cover: text and fades adapt to each album.

Three voices for the title

Keep the original feel, go crisp with Inter Semibold, or soften the edges with Nunito Bold. Choose Track Font under Disco Options, Music Screen.

A shard on the rim opens your menu.

No home screen full of buttons. A small shard sits on the edge; tap it and a circle slides out with your sections. Tap the top or bottom to step through them, the middle to open one, or hold it to go back to Music.

  • Your sections, your order: Music, Library, Search, Queue, EQ, Weather, Apps and more.
  • Gestures: back from the left edge, Music from the bottom, Quick Settings from the top.
  • Hold anything (a song, album, artist, file or folder) for what you can do with it.
  • Lists follow the curve and an A to Z jump gets you anywhere; search shows artists first.

Play by mood.

Place your music on a sphere, from Calm to Energetic and from Dark to Bright. Then drag to the mood you're in and play up to 40 songs from near that spot, in Shuffle, kept as a playlist if you like.

Place it where you are

Hold an album or an artist in the Library and choose Mood, or tap the mood pill while a song plays. Drag the marker, save.

Play what fits

Open SongSphere, drag to a spot and play: a few songs per album and artist, recent plays less likely.

Built in since 2.2. Placements stay on your Disc; turn it off in Settings, Playback, SongSphere.

How SongSphere works

Your music, in numbers.

How long you listened this week, month or year and how that compares, your top artists, albums and songs, the song on repeat, and when you listen, round the rim. Swipe back through time; tap a #1 for its Wrapped.

A song counts after 30 seconds; only the time it really played adds up. Nothing leaves the Disc.

Apps live in the circle.

Add Apps to the navigation circle and pick up to five, each with its icon. Every app opens on a landing page first, so stepping through the circle never starts anything by accident.

  • Choose apps: tap to add or remove; the number is the order.
  • Any installed app can also sit in the circle on its own, or in Shortcuts.
  • App makers: an icon.bmp in the app's folder gives it its icon.
Built in

Boomshine

Dots drift across the round screen. Set off a single blast and every dot it catches bursts too, across twenty levels that only get busier. NeoN rings on black, or Pop! pastels. Your music keeps playing.

From Windows to your Disc, in four clicks.

An app checks your PC, sets up everything it needs, flashes the Disc and later puts new versions on your SD card, signed with your own keys.

Download for Windows Read the full guide

Windows 11, 64-bit, about 10 GB free, FiiO firmware V2.57. The same installer and safety checks as on Linux, with a backup of your Disc's original system. The app isn't code-signed yet: if Windows says it protected your PC, choose More info, then Run anyway.

Install on Linux.

You need a Snowsky Disc on FiiO firmware V2.57, FiiO's official V2.57 firmware ZIP and a good USB data cable. Twenty quiet minutes. On Windows, the app does it all.

  1. Download

    Get the latest release or clone the repository for the installer and signing tools. The repository includes the ready-built interface as payload/mq_ui. Already installed with an update key but on a version before 1.4.1? Flash once more with that same key; later updates use the SD steps below.

    git clone https://github.com/zmd22/diskos-disco.git
cd diskos-disco
./install.sh                     # first time only: host setup

  2. Make your update keys (once, optional)

    Keys let you install later builds from the SD card. If you already have trusted owner keys, reuse them: a new root key isn't trusted by your current install. Keep private keys on your computer and back them up. Skip this and leave out --ota-key if you only want to flash.

    sh ui/tools/owner-keys/diskos-keys.sh       # creates ./diskos-keys/
cp diskos-keys/diskos-root.pub.pem payload/

  3. Flash

    Power the Disc off, hold Volume Down and plug in USB. Before you answer y, check that the ota: ON … key sha256 line matches your key.

    ./diskos-installer install \
  --firmware SNOWSKY_DISC_update_20260909_v257.zip \
  --variant public \
  --ui payload/mq_ui \
  --ota-key payload/diskos-root.pub.pem

  4. Check and sign an update

    Download mq_ui and SHA256SUMS from the same release into one folder and check the binary. For the included repository binary, use payload/mq_ui.

    awk '$2 == "mq_ui" { print }' SHA256SUMS | sha256sum -c -

    Expect mq_ui: OK. Then sign it with the keys matching the root key on your Disc:

    sh ui/tools/owner-keys/diskos-sign.sh \
  /path/to/mq_ui 2.2.0 \
  /absolute/path/to/your/diskos-keys ./diskos-update

    The output holds six files. Every signing run gets a later number than the one before (kept with your keys), so a repeat update is always accepted.

  5. Copy, eject and install

    Copy the whole diskos-update folder to the SD card's top level (<SD>/diskos-update/mq_ui), eject, then on the Disc choose Settings › System › Maintenance › Update from SD Card › Update and restart when prompted. UI updates don't install the separate apps.

  6. Test and keep the trial

    Check Settings › System › About for the new version (Disco! 2.2.0). Keep it running for at least three minutes and play music; it can be accepted automatically, or choose Keep when asked. Go back and a restart revert it. An unproven trial rolls back after two trial boots.

    The full SD update guide covers key checks, trials, rollback and troubleshooting.

Testing release, as is, no liability. Flashing rewrites the Disc's system; a power cut or an interrupted flash can leave it unbootable. Back up your music and keep the saved stock image safe. Never share generated diskos_*.bin images: they contain FiiO's firmware.
