# SongSphere 1.0

Play by mood. Place your albums and artists on a sphere, from Calm to Energetic and from Dark to Bright, then drag
to a mood and play up to 40 songs from near that spot, in Shuffle, kept as a playlist if you like.

A separate app for diskOS Disco! 2.1.0 or later (it uses 2.1.0's app hand-over to play). Its placements live in
`sphere.db` in its own folder; the library is only read. See [install](../../docs/SONGSPHERE_INSTALL.md).
